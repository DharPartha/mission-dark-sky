# Impact Metrics Specification

## Purpose
Show the consequences of different lighting strategies inside the simulation.

## Metrics
- Lighting level
- Estimated energy use
- Estimated operating cost
- Safety/activity status
- Dark-sky/environment indicator
- Change versus baseline

## Comparison Modes
Where practical, compare:
1. Fixed lighting
2. Timed lighting
3. Adaptive lighting

Optional later:
4. Zoned lighting
5. Solar + adaptive concept

## Important
Energy and cost values are estimates based on simulation assumptions.
They must be clearly labeled as simulated estimates.

## Dashboard
Show a small number of meaningful values rather than overwhelming the user.

Example:
- Current Lighting: 62%
- Estimated Energy: 18 units
- Estimated Cost: 4 units
- Activity: 3 people / 1 vehicle
