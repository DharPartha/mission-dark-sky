# Mission Dark Sky: Smart Town — Project Vision

## Tagline
Make Every Light Count

## Core Question
Can inexpensive sensors and adaptive algorithms make existing community lighting smarter—
providing the right amount of light when people need it while reducing unnecessary energy use,
cost, and environmental impact?

## Product Goal
Mission Dark Sky: Smart Town is an interactive Roblox engineering simulation that demonstrates
adaptive community lighting.

The project models a community where outdoor lighting responds to changing conditions instead
of operating at one fixed level.

## Core Loop
Sensors & Conditions -> Adaptive Lighting Engine -> Lighting Adjustment -> Measure Impact -> Adapt

## Inputs
- People
- Vehicles
- Time
- Ambient light
- Weather
- Events/activity
- Environmental sensitivity

## Outputs
- Lighting level
- Energy estimate
- Operating-cost estimate
- Environmental impact indicators
- Safety/activity status

## Product Principle
The goal is NOT simply to make the community darker.

The goal is to explore how lighting can become:
- safer
- more responsive
- more energy efficient
- cost conscious
- environmentally responsible

## Initial Scope
Build one working parking-lot lighting zone before expanding to the complete Smart Town.

## Future Areas
- School
- Main street
- Sports field
- Homes
- Store/business
