How it works

  1. In the game, open the Star Journal and choose Community report.
  2. Select all the report text and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and choose Show my report.
  4. Choose Save as PDF and pick “Save as PDF” in the print window.

Your report stays on this device. This page has no account, no sign-in, and sends nothing anywhere. It still works if you lose your internet connection after it loads.
